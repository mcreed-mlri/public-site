Legal Aid Litigation Skills

For attorneys preparing and trying a case, from the first client interview to the closing argument.

  • Building the case

    Interview clients, investigate the facts, and develop a case theory.

  • Preparing for trial

    Prepare witnesses and exhibits, and plan your examinations.

  • Examining witnesses

    Prepare and conduct direct and cross examinations.

  • In the courtroom

    Make and answer objections, and give openings and closings.
